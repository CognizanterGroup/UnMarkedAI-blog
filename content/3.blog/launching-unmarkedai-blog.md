We launched the UnmarkedAI blog for one simple reason: teams building with AI need a place where product thinking and technical execution meet clearly.

There is no shortage of noise around generative AI. What is harder to find is honest writing about the tradeoffs that matter in production:

- How do you help users trust what an AI-assisted product is doing?
- What should be visible to users and what should stay behind the scenes?
- How do you improve velocity without creating brittle workflows?

This publication will focus on those questions.

## What to expect here

You can expect short, practical posts on topics like:

- building trustworthy AI content flows
- designing product affordances around automation
- operational lessons from shipping AI features
- editorial standards for AI-assisted publishing

We want the writing here to be useful to operators, founders, product teams, and engineers who need something more actionable than trend summaries.

## Why this lives as its own microfrontend

This blog is deployed as a dedicated child app under `/blog`. That gives us room to iterate on publishing, SEO, and content experience independently while still living under the main UnmarkedAI domain.

It is a small architectural decision, but it reflects the same principle we apply to product work: separate concerns cleanly, and make the boundaries legible.

## The direction from here

Our next posts will focus on trust signals, content operations, and how to make AI-supported workflows feel dependable instead of opaque.

If that is the work you are doing too, you are in the right place.
